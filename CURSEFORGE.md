### ✨ 100% Clean-Room & Original Implementation (v1.0.4+)
- **Standalone Engine**: *Zoltraak: Cinematic Edition* runs completely autonomously without requiring external VFX libraries (Photon or KilaGraph).
- **Custom Procedural Geometry**: All 3D vector meshes, logarithmic inward spiral rings, aerodynamic beam ribbons, and geodesic barrier domes are computed dynamically in real-time via Blaze3D & JOML.
- **100% Original GLSL 150 Core Shaders**: All shaders (`zoltraak_beam`, `black_zoltraak_beam`, `great_zoltraak_white`, `great_zoltraak_black`, `great_zoltraak_plume`, `corona`, `rapid_zoltraak_bullet`, `zoltraak_impact`, `magic_circle`, `defense_barrier`, `gargantua`, `cinematic_flare`, and `unsealing_fire_column`) and textures are custom-crafted and authored from scratch.
- Detailed legal declarations and clean-room documentation can be inspected in **[NOTICE.md](https://github.com/ling-gwdgw2/Zoltraak-Cinematic-NeoForge-1.21.1/blob/main/NOTICE.md)**.
