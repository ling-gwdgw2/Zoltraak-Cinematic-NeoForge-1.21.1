2. CLEAN-ROOM ORIGINALITY STATEMENT
-----------------------------------
As of Version 1.0.4 and all subsequent releases, Zoltraak: Cinematic Edition
is an entirely independent, 100% clean-room original creation.

All Java source code, procedural vector mesh algorithms (Blaze3D / JOML),
dedicated GLSL 150 shaders, JSON configurations, and textures are custom-built
and authored from the ground up without using third-party proprietary code.

Core Shaders (100% Custom):
  - cinematic_photon_beam (.fsh, .vsh, .json)
  - cinematic_abyssal_beam (.fsh, .vsh, .json)
  - cinematic_great_photon (.fsh, .vsh, .json)
  - cinematic_great_abyssal (.fsh, .vsh, .json)
  - cinematic_plasma_plume (.fsh, .vsh, .json)
  - cinematic_singularity_corona (.fsh, .vsh, .json)
  - rapid_zoltraak_bullet (.fsh, .vsh, .json)
  - magic_circle & black_magic_circle (.fsh, .vsh, .json)
  - zoltraak_impact (.fsh, .vsh, .json)
  - cinematic_flare (.fsh, .vsh, .json)
  - defense_barrier (.fsh, .vsh, .json)
  - gargantua (.fsh, .vsh, .json)
  - unsealing_fire_column (.fsh, .vsh, .json)

Textures & GUI Icons:
  - zoltraak_glow.png: Procedural HDR radial bloom texture
  - zoltraak_magic_circle.png: High-definition 2048x2048 custom artwork
  - defense.png: Custom 64x64 hexagonal mana barrier spell icon

Audio Sound Effects (100% In-House Synthesized):
  - cinematic_charge.ogg: Mathematically synthesized rising mana swell
  - cinematic_fire.ogg: Supersonic laser transient & photonic plasma hiss
  - cinematic_great_fire.ogg: Sub-bass seismic roar & cavernous reverb wash
  - cinematic_impact.ogg: Kinetic fracture pop & crater shockwave rumble
